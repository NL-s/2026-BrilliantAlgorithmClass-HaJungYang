A friendly cartoon visualization of Merge Sort for Korean high school students. Depict a cheerful librarian character holding two small stacks of sorted cards, comparing the top card of each stack and placing the smaller one into a new sorted pile. Above the librarian, show the full recursive splitting tree: a row of 8 colorful numbered cards splits into pairs, then singles, and arrows flow back up as they merge into sorted groups. Use warm pastel colors and clear directional arrows. Each merge step should be highlighted with a glowing outline. Include a small label "O(n log n)" in a speech bubble.
