Below this, three horizontal arrows show the three range update operations: a green arrow spanning indices 1-3 labeled "+5", a blue arrow spanning indices 2-4 labeled "+3", and an orange arrow spanning indices 3-6 labeled "+2". Connecting vertical arrows between the diff row and the result row are labeled "prefix sum".
